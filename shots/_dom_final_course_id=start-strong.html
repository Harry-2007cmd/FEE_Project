Start strong

A steady, confidence-building introduction to strength training.

What you'll learn

  • Move through the five foundation lifts with good form
  • Build a simple 3-day weekly strength routine
  • Choose a starting weight and know when to add more
  • Warm up and cool down so you feel good afterwards

Timeline

  1. Days 1–3Learn the patterns

    Squat, hinge, push, pull and carry with just your bodyweight.

  2. Days 4–7Add light load

    Introduce dumbbells or bands and find your starting weight.

  3. Days 8–11Build the routine

    Put the patterns into three short full-body sessions.

  4. Days 12–14Test and plan

    Repeat session one, notice the progress and plan your next month.
