FAQs

Everything you need to know before starting

Is flexform for beginners?

Yes. Every plan is designed to be approachable, with guided sessions and flexible routines that work for different experience levels.

Do I need a strict routine?

No. The platform is built around realistic progress. Small, sustainable habits matter more than perfection.

Can I try coaching before committing?

Absolutely. You can explore the programs, preview coach styles, and create your account before choosing a plan.

What makes flexform different?

We combine supportive coaching, thoughtful tools, and a healthier relationship with movement—so your routine fits your life, not the other way around.
